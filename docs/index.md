# Home Buying, Calm: A First-Timer's Manual

Welcome. If you are buying your first home, you probably have a folder (or a shoebox) full of paperwork you don't fully understand, and a low hum of anxiety about whether some stranger is about to take advantage of you.

This manual is the antidote. It explains every major document, every major step, and every major decision in **plain English** — the way a patient friend would, not the way a mortgage brochure would.

## Who this is for

- First-time buyers who want to understand what they are signing.
- Anyone who has stood in an open house and thought, "I have no idea what I'm looking at."
- People who want to budget realistically, not hopefully.

## How to use this manual

1. Skim the **Glossary** once so words stop feeling scary.
2. Read **Pre-approval Without Panic** before you talk to any lender.
3. Bring the **Tour Checklist** (printed or on your phone) to every showing.
4. Re-read the chapter for whichever step you're currently living through.
5. Print or save the **Closing-Day Grid** the week before you close.

## The one rule of this manual

> **Education only.** Nothing here is financial advice, legal advice, tax advice, or a recommendation to buy, wait, or refinance. Decisions about your money and your home are yours, ideally made with licensed professionals you have chosen. This guide just makes sure the words they use don't sound like a foreign language.

If you have read this far, you are already more prepared than most first-time buyers. Deep breath. Let's begin.
